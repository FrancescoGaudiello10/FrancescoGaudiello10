# 🌦️ PolyWeather

> A clearer view of temperature forecasting markets.

PolyWeather is a map-first dashboard for reading weather forecasts alongside temperature-market probabilities.

## The idea

Weather decisions are time-sensitive and scattered across several views. PolyWeather brings forecast windows, observed conditions, market-implied probabilities, and historical snapshots together so a city can be understood at a glance.

`forecasts + observations + market odds + history → decision context`

## What it brings together

- 🗺️ A map-driven view of cities and predicted temperature buckets.
- 🌡️ Standard forecasts and intraday updates.
- 📡 Reality checks from observed weather data.
- 📊 Persistent snapshots to compare how conditions and probabilities evolve.
- 🛠️ An administrative workflow with an audit trail for data jobs.
