# 📝 ClickInail

> An assisted workflow for structured administrative form completion.

ClickInail focuses on reducing the repetitive and error-prone parts of completing structured INAIL forms.

## The idea

The project combines explicit rules, guided browser automation, and optional reuse of prior answers to make repeatable form workflows easier to manage. It is designed around clear modes for simulation and scheduled test scenarios.

## What it focuses on

- ✅ Rule-driven assistance for consistent answers.
- 🔁 Optional reuse of structured historical responses.
- 🤖 Browser automation for repetitive interaction steps.
- 🧪 Separate workflows for everyday simulation and scheduled tests.
