## Selected work

### [PokemonDropAlert](https://www.pokedropalert.com)
Keeping Pokemon collectors in Italy ahead of drops, preorders, and restocks.
Signals from multiple shops become a website, real-time Telegram alerts, and community updates.

### [ANDV Hantavirus Tracker](https://www.andvhantavirus.com)
A live intelligence tracker that brings sources, data, an interactive map, and updates into one clear public view. An automated pipeline collects reports, extracts useful signals, and keeps the tracker current.

### Instagram Export Intelligence
A privacy-first, local analytics workspace for official Instagram/Meta exports.
Raw archives become normalized data, historical snapshots, reports, and a local dashboard -- without passwords, scraping, or uploading personal data. Built around DuckDB, it makes personal data inspectable without giving it away.

### PolyWeather
A map-first decision dashboard for temperature forecasting markets.
It combines forecasts, observed weather data, market probabilities, and historical snapshots to make fast-changing information easier to read.
